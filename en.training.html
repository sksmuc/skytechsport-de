Skiing and snowboarding in the heart of Munich

State-of-the-art technology and detailed virtual slopes
— for a descent that feels like being on the mountain.

Train at the SkyTechSport Club in Munich and enjoy spectacular descents all year round:
Slopes, slalom routes and alpine routes on a panoramic screen are waiting for you! For realistic driving fun and numerous challenges, you can also integrate hills and moguls or adjust the conditions for more effective training. Numerous sensors track the position and movement of your skis or board, while software and motors realistically simulate the g-force between the board and the snow.

Our training is suitable for:

Beginners:

Familiarize yourself with the basics of skiing and snowboarding in just a few hours, warm and dry. You will then conquer the slopes with more confidence.

Advanced:

Put the finishing touches on your skiing and snowboarding skills with the simulator and get on the slopes with even more techniques for your next descent.

Professionals & Athletes:

Train effectively on your skiing technique for slalom, giant slalom or alpine all year round and prepare yourself for the best possible result for your next competition.

Intense workout:

Train all muscles on the simulator with special workout programs and improve your fitness and technique.

Rehabilitation after injuries:

With us you can get back to your best shape more quickly after injuries under controlled conditions by increasing the load in training step by step.

Course of a training session

For beginners, we recommend a 30-minute introductory session.

In 15 minutes, a trained athlete can complete up to 15 runs on our simulator without a break. Beginners, on the other hand, usually can't do more than 5 minutes at a time. That's why we're taking it slow! Our professional SkyTech coaches will help you and adjust the simulator settings according to your wishes and ability.
