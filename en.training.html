Skiing and snowboarding in the heart of Munich

Latest technology and detailed virtual slopes
— for a descent that feels like on the mountain.

Train at the SkyTechSport Club in Munich and enjoy spectacular descents all year round:
Slopes, slalom routes and alpine routes on a panoramic screen are waiting for you! For realistic driving pleasure and numerous challenges, you can also integrate hills and mogul slopes or adjust the conditions for more effective training. Numerous sensors track the position and movement of your skis or board, while software and motors really mimic the g-force between board and snow.

Our training is suitable for:

Beginners:

Familiarize yourself with the basics of skiing and snowboarding in just a few hours in warm and dry. Then you conquer the slope with more driving safety.

Advanced:

Put the finishing touches on your ski and snowboard skills with the simulator and get on the slopes with even more techniques for the next descent.

Professionals & athletes:

Train effectively on your driving technique for slalom, giant slalom or alpine all year round and prepare for the best possible result for your next competition.

Intensive workout:

Train all muscles on the simulator with special workout programs and improve your condition and technique.

Rehabilitation after injuries:

With us, you will find your way back to your best form faster after injuries under controlled conditions by increasing the load in training step by step.

Procedure of a training session

In 15 minutes, a trained athlete manages up to 15 slope runs without a break on our simulator. Beginners, on the other hand, usually do not manage more than 5 minutes at a time. That's why we're taking it slow! Our professional SkyTech coaches will help you and adjust the settings of the simulator according to your wishes and skills.
